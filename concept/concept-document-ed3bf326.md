# MaMarket Stock — Concept Document

## 1. Executive Summary

MaMarket Stock is a greenfield, standalone, single-machine, web-based stock management application designed for retail markets. It is specifically developed for the staff and administrators of retail market operations to manage product inventory effectively. The application addresses the limitations of manual or spreadsheet-based inventory tracking by providing a structured, auditable batch ledger and an intuitive conversational interface for product catalogue management, grounded in the strategic decisions from the project's convergence phase.

## 2. Problem Statement & Opportunity

Retail market staff currently face significant operational challenges in tracking stock across diverse product categories, including drinks, food, cheese, meats, ice creams, and all barcoded products, due to the absence of a dedicated inventory management tool. Existing methods, such as spreadsheets and manual counts, are prone to human error, lack real-time visibility, and fail to provide structured reorder signals, leading to potential stockouts or overstocking. The initial project brief's implicit assumption of live real-time tracking was refined during the challenge dialogue, leading to the strategic decision that batch-interval updates are operationally sufficient and architecturally simpler for this context. This refinement highlights a clear opportunity to deliver a reliable, auditable inventory management loop as a validated prototype, enhancing operational efficiency and data integrity without unnecessary complexity.

## 3. Target Users & Pain Points

MaMarket Stock is designed for two distinct user roles: Administrator and Staff, with no customer-facing interface or external system integrations.

**Administrator:**
*   **Role:** Manages the product catalogue, configures reorder thresholds, uploads sold-items files, and monitors overall inventory health.
*   **Pain Points:** Currently lacks a structured, in-app method for product catalogue maintenance, relying on manual processes. There is no automated mechanism to set and adjust reorder thresholds, leading to reactive stock management.

**Staff:**
*   **Role:** Primarily responsible for uploading sold-items files and viewing the inventory, acting on low-stock alerts.
*   **Pain Points:** Experiences a lack of structured tools to decrement stock based on sold-items data, leading to manual updates or delays. There is no automatic, clear low-stock signal, requiring constant manual checks to identify products needing replenishment.

Both roles suffer from the absence of a unified system, leading to data inconsistencies and inefficient workflows in managing the product catalogue and inventory levels.

## 4. Proposed Solution

MaMarket Stock offers a streamlined solution to retail market inventory management through a set of core features:

*   **Chat-style product catalogue entry:** Users can easily add new products by typing the product name, price, and barcode. The system then prompts for the initial quantity, and the stock item is created in SQLite. Barcodes can be entered manually or via a hardware scanner for efficiency.
*   **Sold-items file upload:** The application supports both CSV and Excel formats for sold-items data. The system parses the uploaded file, matches products by their barcode, and decrements quantities in the SQLite database accordingly.
*   **Inventory view:** A tabular display provides real-time (post-upload) visibility of all products with their current quantities. This view refreshes immediately after each upload or catalogue operation, ensuring up-to-date information.
*   **Low-stock alert:** A clear visual badge or colour change is applied to any product whose quantity falls below its pre-configured reorder threshold. This threshold is admin-configurable per product, with a sensible default applied at product creation and editable thereafter.
*   **Audit trail:** Each upload event is logged with a timestamp and file reference, providing a complete and auditable history of stock movements for traceability and accountability.

**Value Proposition:** MaMarket Stock delivers a disciplined, auditable batch inventory ledger that aligns perfectly with the natural rhythm of retail market operations. Stock is loaded at the beginning of a period, sold-items are uploaded at the end of a shift or day, and the inventory view instantly reflects the current state. The conversational product entry interface eliminates the need for a separate, complex catalogue management screen, significantly lowering the barrier to onboarding new products and enhancing user adoption.

## 5. Technical Approach (High-Level)

The MaMarket Stock application will be implemented as a monolithic single-machine web application, leveraging a robust and widely supported technology stack.

**Architecture:** The system will operate as a standalone, single-user application on a local machine, eliminating the need for network sharing or complex distributed systems.

**Frontend:** Developed using React, the frontend will function as a single-page application (SPA), served locally. This ensures a responsive and dynamic user experience without external server dependencies.

**Backend:** A Node.js server (utilizing a framework like Express) will handle all backend logic, API interactions, and data processing. This server will run as a local process on the deployment machine.

**Database:** SQLite will serve as the database. Its file-based nature requires no separate server process, making it an ideal choice for single-user, single-machine deployments where simplicity and local data storage are paramount.

**File Parsing:** For handling sold-items uploads, Node.js libraries such as `csv-parser` for CSV files and `xlsx` for Excel files will be integrated. These libraries will facilitate robust parsing, column mapping, and comprehensive error reporting to manage variations in input file formats.

**Chat Interface:** The conversational product-entry interface will be implemented as a stateful React component. This component will manage the dialogue flow and drive a sequential product-creation API process on the backend, ensuring a guided and intuitive user experience.

**Barcode Input:** Barcode input will be managed through standard keyboard input. Hardware scanners typically emulate keyboard entry, thus requiring no special drivers or SDKs and simplifying integration.

**Dependencies:** The solution is designed to be entirely self-contained. There will be no external APIs, no dedicated authentication service, and no cloud dependencies. The application will function completely on the local machine, ensuring data privacy and operational independence.

## 6. Competitive Positioning

MaMarket Stock distinguishes itself from existing alternatives through several key differentiators, specifically designed to meet the unique operational context of retail markets.

**Vs. Spreadsheets:** MaMarket Stock provides a structured data model with barcode-keyed products, eliminating the manual lookup and formula errors inherent in spreadsheet-based systems. It automates stock decrement from uploaded files, removing the need for error-prone manual subtraction and enhancing data accuracy.

**Vs. Generic Inventory SaaS Tools:** Unlike subscription-based SaaS solutions, MaMarket Stock is fully standalone, requiring no ongoing subscription fees or internet dependency. This ensures data remains on the local machine, addressing privacy concerns and operational continuity in environments with unreliable internet access.

**Vs. Full ERP/POS Systems:** The application boasts zero integration overhead, being deployable on a single PC without requiring complex IT infrastructure. This contrasts sharply with the significant setup and maintenance costs associated with comprehensive ERP or POS systems.

**Strategic Alternatives Considered:** During Phase 1, three strategic alternatives were evaluated:

*   **Alternative A "Batch Ledger" (Chosen):** This approach, focusing on file-upload-driven, auditable stock management, was selected because it directly aligns with the confirmed operational model of end-of-shift uploads. It minimizes implementation complexity for the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) and naturally produces an auditable event log, supporting the project's lean startup principles and workflow validation goal.
*   **Alternative B "Live Scanner Hub" (Rejected):** This alternative proposed using a scanner as a live decrement channel. It was rejected due to its higher complexity, the inherent reliability risks of live scanner integration for an MVP, and the significant behavioral change it would require from staff. The confirmed operational model did not necessitate live, real-time accuracy, making this approach overly ambitious for the prototype phase.
*   **Alternative C "Inventory Intelligence Layer" (Rejected):** This option involved advanced features like pattern recognition and predictive reordering. It was deemed premature and out of scope for the MVP, as it requires substantial historical data that does not yet exist and focuses on analytical sophistication rather than the core workflow correctness targeted by the prototype.

The decision to proceed with the "Batch Ledger + Chat Catalogue" strategy was a deliberate outcome of the structured dialogue, prioritizing simplicity, auditability, and alignment with actual operational needs.
